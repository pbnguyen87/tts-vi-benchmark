The three differences that matter most for Vietnamese

  • Tokenizer. ZONOS2's UTF-8 bytes need no change for Vietnamese diacritics. Confucius4's Mistral BPE splits one syllable into about 2.9 tokens, so sequences are longer and text has to be cut into 80-token segments.
  • Speaker information. Confucius4 has two paths, embedding and mel prompt, so it keeps timbre and style better when the reference is clean. ZONOS2 has a single vector: less sensitive to noise, but also less detailed.
  • Acoustic stage. Confucius4 needs an extra S2A fine-tune for natural Vietnamese prosody. ZONOS2 is one model, so one LoRA pass is enough, but it needs a larger GPU because of its 8B parameters.
